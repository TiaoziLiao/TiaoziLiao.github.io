## About Me

Chao Liao is a researcher at ByteDance Seed, working on multimodal foundation models. His research began with **multimodal large language models (MLLMs)** and then moved to **unified models for multimodal understanding and generation**, with the goal of exploring new paradigms for multimodal pre-training. At Seed, he has contributed to unified models such as **Mogao** and **Seedream 4.0 / 5.0**. He currently works on **world models** and **world action models**, aiming to extend multimodal foundation models from perceiving and generating the world to predicting and acting in it.
